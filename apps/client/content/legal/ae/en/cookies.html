This Cookie Policy describes how Selfieface uses cookies and similar storage on the United Arab Emirates (AED) storefront.

Essential cookies

  • Bag / guest cart — keeps your bag and guest identity so checkout works.
  • Locale (selfieface_locale) — remembers your language.
  • Market / currency (selfieface_currency) — shows the correct prices.
  • Theme — light or dark preference when set.
  • Consent choice — stores whether you accepted analytics cookies.

Essential cookies are required for the shop to function and are always active.

Analytics (optional)

If you choose Accept all, we may record page views, searches, and product clicks to improve merchandising. Choosing Essential only disables this tracking. Checkout still works either way.

Managing preferences

Clear site data or change your choice by clearing the consent cookie/localStorage key selfieface_cookie_consent and reloading the page to see the banner again.
