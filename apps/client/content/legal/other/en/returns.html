This page outlines returns and exchanges for orders placed on the Selfieface International (USD) storefront.

Eligibility

Unopened products in original condition may be eligible within a limited window after delivery. Hygiene-sensitive items may be excluded. Contact support with your order number to start a request.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method where possible. Detailed refund rules (including partial refunds) will be refined in a later admin configuration phase.
